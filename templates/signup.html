Security & Service Rules Step 1 of 5

1. Zero-Knowledge Cryptography

All encryption keys and master passwords are derived locally in your browser. The server never stores, receives, or has access to your passwords or decrypted files.

⚠️ 2. Irrecoverable Credentials (CRITICAL)

Since this service is zero-knowledge, there is no password reset mechanism. If you forget your master password, all vault data is lost permanently and cannot be recovered by support or administrators.

3. Data & Storage Limits

Default account limits allow up to 1,000 records and 100 MB of total vault storage. Storage usage is calculated based on raw client-side ciphertext length.

4. Acceptable Content Policy

You agree not to store malicious software, illegal material, or use the storage service for unauthorized activities.

5. Service Availability Agreement

The vault is provided as-is without warranties of uptime. Continued use of the platform constitutes agreement to all these rules.
